Beginner Quick Guide

To lead on the pitch, you need both positive commands (what to do) and negative commands (what NOT to do):

  • Positive Command (Do it!): Use the él/ella present form.
    Aguantar → ¡Aguanta! (Hold/Delay!) • Cubrir → ¡Cubre! (Cover!) • Ir → ¡Ve! (Go!) • Salir → ¡Sal! (Step out!)
  • Negative Command (Don't do it!): Put No + switch the vowel (-AR → -es; -ER/-IR → -as).
    Entrar → ¡No entres! (Don't dive in!) • Perder → ¡No pierdas el balón! (Don't lose the ball!) • Salir → ¡No salgas! (Don't step out!)

1. ¡ el balón rápido al otro lado! (mover - positive)

2. ¡No faltas tontas cerca del área! (hacer - negative)

3. ¡ la espalda del lateral! (cubrir - positive)

4. ¡No solo al portero! (dejar - negative)

5. ¡ atento al rechace! (estar - irregular positive: estate / sé)

Part 3: Pressure Situations

1. Your center-back is 1v1 against a fast winger. Tell him to hold his position and not dive in:

Sample Pitch Answer: ¡Aguanta ahí, no entres! ¡Espera ayuda!

2. Your teammate has the ball on the edge of the box with space. Tell him to shoot and not pass:

Sample Pitch Answer: ¡Tira a puerta, no la pases!

3. You are defending a 1-0 lead in the 90th minute. Give two commands to keep the ball:

Sample Pitch Answer: ¡Mueve el balón rápido y no pierdas la posesión!

Part 4: Pitch Audio / Voice Note Prep

Speaking Drill: Deliver 5 positive and negative command pairs with match intensity.
